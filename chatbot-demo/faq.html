Frequently asked questions

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday 8 am – 10 pm, Sunday 9 am – 6 pm. We're closed on Diwali and Holi.

Do you deliver?

Yes, within 5 km via our website, Swiggy and Zomato. Orders usually arrive in 30–40 minutes.

Do you have vegan or gluten-free options?

Oat and almond milk at no extra cost, vegan banana bread and gluten-free brownies.

Can I book a table?

Call +91 90000 00000 or use "Book a table". Groups of 8+ please book a day ahead.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, all major cards and UPI.

Do you sell coffee beans?

Yes, 250 g bags of our house blend, whole or ground to order.

Are pets allowed?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in our outdoor seating area.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, from ₹500, available at the counter.

Are you hiring?

Send your CV to jobs@brewandbean.example.
